# Nexus Watch (Wear OS)

Companion app for **Wear OS 3+** watches (e.g. Samsung Galaxy Watch 4 and newer, Pixel Watch). It talks directly to the
VRC Nexus Windows app on your home network – no phone app and no cloud service needed.

## Features

- ♥ **Heart rate from the watch sensor** → VRC Nexus heart-rate source “VRC Nexus Watch” (avatar parameters, chatbox `{puls}`, HUD …)
- 💤 Sleep mode on/off
- 🔞 Only when NSFW is unlocked in VRC Nexus: panic button, all-clear, private mode, consent light 🟢🟡🔴, stop toys
- Shows the current world, player count and heart rate
- 🧩 **Tiles** (since 8.2): “Nexus – heart rate & status”, “Nexus – quick actions” (sleep mode, chatbox quick texts, macros) and 🔞 ***
- ⌚ **Complications** (since 8.2) for any watch face: heart rate (watch sensor or the source VRC Nexus uses), VRChat status (friends online / world) and 🔞 ***
- 💬 Chatbox quick texts (editable on the PC), ⚡ PC macros and avatar presets, ⚠ heart-rate alarm, 🕶 discreet mode

## Pairing

1. In VRC Nexus open **💓 Puls**, enable **VRC Nexus Watch** and note the 6-digit code.
2. On the watch tap **Koppeln** and enter the code. The watch finds the PC automatically (UDP broadcast, port 17873);
   if not, enter the address shown in VRC Nexus (`IP:17874`).
3. Each watch gets its own key (stored on the PC only as a SHA-256 hash) and can be removed in VRC Nexus at any time.
4. Since 8.2 a watch takes over the PC's 18+ clearance when it is paired (only if the PC itself is unlocked). The clearance is
   bound to that pairing and is removed by unpairing, by any panic and when 18+ is switched off on the PC; it can be
   transferred again under **Tools → 📡 Devices**. Unpairing on the watch also removes it on the PC.

The watch and the PC must be on the same network. The app asks for Wi-Fi because the Bluetooth link through the phone
often cannot reach devices on the home network.

## Build

Plain Java. Since 8.2 the app uses AndroidX libraries from Google for tiles and complications (pinned:
`androidx.wear.tiles:tiles:1.4.1`, `androidx.wear.protolayout:protolayout(-expression):1.2.1`,
`androidx.concurrent:concurrent-futures:1.1.0`, `androidx.wear.watchface:watchface-complications-data-source:1.2.1`).
`gradle -p apps/vrc-nexus-watch assembleRelease` (Android SDK 34, JDK 17+).
CI builds it together with the Android app and attaches `VRC-Nexus-Watch-<version>-wearos.apk` to the release.
Install with `adb install` (watch in developer mode with Wi-Fi debugging) or a sideload tool.


## Watch face (`watchface/`)

Since 8.1.2 there is a second, code-free module: the **VRC Nexus watch face** in Watch Face Format 1 (`res/raw/watchface.xml`, Wear OS 4+, minSdk 33). It shows time, seconds (hidden in ambient), date, heart rate, a battery arc and two customizable complication slots (defaults: steps, battery). Since 8.2 the watch face editor offers a background (six built-in images generated by `watchface/tools/make_backgrounds.py`, or black – hidden in ambient mode), a colour theme (six palettes) and a seconds toggle; the complication slots also render ranged values as rings. CI validates the XML against Google's official WFF schema (`watchface/tools/validate_wff.py`) before building. Own photos are not offered: the WFF `PhotosConfiguration` needs Wear OS 6, while this face supports Wear OS 4+. The app's own face (Nexus Watch app, incl. backgrounds uploaded from the PC) stays available. It is built by its own CI job (`gradle -p apps/vrc-nexus-watch :watchface:assembleRelease`) and attached to the release as `VRC-Nexus-WatchFace-<version>-wearos.apk`. The PC app's watch installer installs it together with the watch app and tries to make it the active watch face (`am broadcast -a com.google.android.wearable.app.DEBUG_SURFACE --es operation set-watchface`).
